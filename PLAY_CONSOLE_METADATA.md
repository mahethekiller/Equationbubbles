- **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?** `Yes` (HTTPS/TLS).
- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?** `Yes` (via email contact in Privacy Policy).

### Specific Data Type Declarations

| Data Category | Data Type | Collected? | Shared? | Purpose | Ephemeral? |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|:---:|
| **Device or other IDs** | Device ID, Advertising ID | Yes | Yes (Google AdMob) | Advertising & Analytics, Fraud Prevention | Yes |
| **App Info & Performance** | Crash logs, Diagnostics | Yes | Yes (Google AdMob) | App functionality, Analytics | Yes |

### Family Policy Declaration (AdMob Specifics)
- **Are advertising identifiers collected for children?**
  - **No.** The app initiates all AdMob requests with:
    - `tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true`
    - `tagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true`
    - `maxAdContentRating = 'G'`
    - `npa = true` (Non-Personalized Ads)
  - Under Google AdMob policy, personalized targeting is completely disabled for child-directed requests.

## 8. Graphic Asset Specifications

Prepare the following visual files before submitting to the Google Play Store:

### 1. App Icon
- **Dimensions:** `512 x 512 px`
- **Format:** 32-bit PNG (with alpha)
- **Max File Size:** `1 MB`
- **Design Guidance:** A vibrant torn-paper light blue bubble featuring `2 + 3` or `5`, pinned onto cardboard with a yellow pushpin and origami paper stars.

### 2. Feature Graphic (Hero Banner)
- **Dimensions:** `1024 x 500 px`
- **Format:** JPG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- **Max File Size:** `15 MB`
- **Design Guidance:** Cardboard craft desk background, bold wooden/paper 3D title *"Equation Bubbles"*, floating paper speech bubbles with glowing math equations (`7+3=10`, `4×3=12`), and celebratory origami confetti. Keep key text within the safe center zone (`800 x 400 px`).

# Privacy Policy for Equation Bubbles

**Last Updated:** September 18, 2026

Equation Bubbles ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to providing an educational, safe, and family-friendly mobile experience. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning information collection, use, and disclosure in connection with our mobile game "Equation Bubbles".

## 1. Compliance with Children's Privacy Laws (COPPA & GDPR-K)
Our application is designed for children of all ages, including children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / GDPR-K) in the European Union.

We do NOT knowingly collect, request, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as names, physical addresses, email addresses, telephone numbers, photographic images, voice recordings, or precise geolocation from children.

## 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device
Equation Bubbles stores player progression data—such as high scores, unlocked levels, star ratings (0–3 stars per level), and sound/music toggle preferences—strictly on your local device using standard HTML5 storage (`localStorage`). This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third-party database.

## 3. Advertising & Third-Party SDK Disclosures
Equation Bubbles contains advertisements powered by Google AdMob. To protect child privacy and comply with Google Play's Designed for Families policy:
- All advertising requests are explicitly tagged with `tagForChildDirectedTreatment = true` and `maxAdContentRating = 'G'`.
- Interest-based (behavioral) targeting, remarketing, and personalized profiling are strictly disabled (`npa = true`).
- Only family-safe, non-personalized contextual ads are served.
- AdMob may collect ephemeral diagnostic data, crash logs, and non-identifying technical device identifiers strictly for fraud prevention, spam detection, and ad delivery metrics as permitted under COPPA.

For more details on Google's privacy practices, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## 4. In-App Purchases and External Links
- Equation Bubbles contains ZERO in-app purchases (IAP) or paid microtransactions.
- All non-gameplay settings and data reset controls are locked behind a parental multiplication gate to prevent unintended actions by young children.
- We do not integrate external social media platforms, forums, or third-party web browsers.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
Because Equation Bubbles does not transmit or maintain user accounts or personal information on external servers, you can completely erase all local game data at any time by clearing the application data in your Android system settings or uninstalling the app.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any modifications will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our child-privacy practices, please reach out to us at:
- **Email:** developer@equationbubbles.com (or your developer contact email)
- **Developer Name:** Mahe The Killer / Equation Bubbles Team
