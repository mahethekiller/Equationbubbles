## 💡 Pro-Tips for Generating & Uploading to Google Play

1. **Aspect Ratios**:
   - For Midjourney: use the `--ar 1:1` for Icon, `--ar 2:1` for Feature Graphic, `--ar 9:16` for Phone Screenshots, and `--ar 16:10` for Tablet.
2. **Text Placement**:
   - Google Play places branding badges and category tags on top of icons and feature graphics. Keep your primary focal points in the central 70% safe zone.
3. **No Alpha on Feature Graphic**:
   - Google Play will reject Feature Graphics that contain alpha/transparency. Export your 1024x500 banner as a high-quality JPG or 24-bit PNG with a solid background.
4. **Legibility at Small Sizes**:
   - The App Icon will be scaled down to 48x48 px on phone app grids. The prompt for the icon emphasizes high-contrast, bold numbers (`2 + 3`) and a clean single-subject composition to maintain recognition at micro sizes.
